# RecallFrame

**Evidence-first video knowledge workspace.** RecallFrame turns a video collection into a private, searchable library where every useful answer leads back to a timestamp in its source.

## Product principles

1. Source evidence stays visible and navigable.
2. Local processing is the default for uploaded media and transcript search.
3. External AI use is disclosed and consented to per video.
4. Empty and unavailable states are honest; never imply that unprocessed sources are indexed.
5. Keep the workbench calm, compact, and legible: dark zinc surfaces, violet focus, Inter UI, and IBM Plex Mono timecodes.
